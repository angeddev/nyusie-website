Account & data

Delete your Nyusie account

This page explains how to delete your account in Nyusie, the audio news app from 17774699 Canada Inc., what happens to your data, and how to request deletion here without the app.

Request deletion on this pageDelete in the app
Have Premium? Cancel your subscription first. Deleting your Nyusie account does not cancel a subscription. Subscriptions are billed by Apple or Google, so you'll keep being charged until you cancel with them: Google Play subscriptions · Cancel an Apple subscription. Founding-member pricing ends when your account is deleted.

Delete your account in the app

On Android

  1. Open Nyusie and go to:
    Settings›Account›Delete account
  2. Confirm that you want to delete your account. Nyusie then opens this page.
  3. Enter your account email, type the 6-digit code we email you, and confirm. That completes the request.

On iPhone

  1. Open Nyusie and go to:
    Settings›Account›Delete account
  2. Confirm that you want to delete your account. That completes the request.

Request by email

Send an email to privacy@nyusie.com from the address on your Nyusie account, asking us to delete it. We'll confirm by reply, and the same 7-day timeline below applies from that confirmation.

Write the email

Can't receive the code? Email privacy@nyusie.com from the address on your account and we'll help.

What happens after you confirm

WhenWhat happens
Straight awayYou're signed out of Nyusie on every device, and your account is scheduled for deletion.
Days 1 to 7Your data is kept so you can change your mind. Sign in to the Nyusie app with the same account and everything is as you left it, with no need to sign up again.
Day 8Your account and all its data are permanently deleted. This can't be undone, and we can no longer help with anything about the old account.

You're welcome back any time. After deletion you can create a new account with the same email address; it starts fresh on the Free plan.

What is deleted

On day 8 we permanently delete everything we hold about your account, including:

  • Your account and profile: name, email address and sign-in details
  • Location settings and the place names used for your briefs
  • Your briefs, deep dives, their audio and transcripts, and your brief history
  • Listening activity: plays, streaks, topic usage and monthly allowance counts
  • Subscription and founding-member records stored by Nyusie
  • Records of the service emails we sent you

What is kept

Nyusie keeps nothing about your account after day 8. Two things sit outside our systems:

  • Store purchase records. If you subscribed, Apple or Google keeps its own record of your purchases under its privacy policy. We can't delete those.
  • Audio on your phone. Briefs already downloaded to your device stay there until you uninstall Nyusie or clear its storage.
