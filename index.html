One brief, five tiers

News in the order it touches your life.

Most news apps start with the biggest headline on the planet. Nyusie starts at your door and works outward, so you hear what matters nearby before the rest of the world.

TIER 1

Local

Your city and region: council votes, transit, schools and what's happening down the street.

TIER 2

Provincial

Your province or state: health, housing, policy and the economy close to home.

TIER 3

National

Your country: the stories that are about home and nowhere else.

TIER 4

Continental

Your neighbours: news from across your continent, and how it lands where you live.

TIER 5

Global

The world: everything beyond your continent, and the events that affect everyone.

How it works

Press play. That's it.

1

It knows where you are

Your location sets your local tier, and it follows you. Live in one city and work in another? Your afternoon brief covers where you are then.

2

Listen to your brief

Stories from established news outlets, written and read aloud by AI. Follow along in the transcript, skip a story, or change the speed.

3

Go deeper when you want

When the brief ends, pick a topic for a short deep dive. Fresh briefs are ready through the day, so you can catch up morning, noon or night.

Topic deep dives
