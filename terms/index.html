3. What Nyusie does

Nyusie creates personalised audio news briefs across five geographic tiers (local, provincial, national, continental and global), topic deep dives, and a weather update for your area. Briefs and deep dives are written by artificial intelligence (AI) from news reports by third-party publishers and read aloud by an AI-generated voice. Your first brief each day opens with a spoken greeting that tells you it is your AI news brief, and the app labels briefs and deep dives as AI-generated.

Nyusie summarises the news using AI, and AI can make mistakes. A brief may contain errors, leave out context, misattribute a statement, mispronounce names, or include outdated information. News stories themselves can also be wrong or change after publication.

  • Briefs are for general information only. They are not professional advice, including financial, investment, legal, medical, safety or emergency advice.
  • Do not rely on a brief for decisions that matter. Check important facts with the original source or a qualified professional.
  • For emergencies, severe weather or public safety alerts, follow official sources and local authorities, not Nyusie.
