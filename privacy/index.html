1. Who we are

Nyusie is an AI audio news app for iOS and Android, made by 17774699 Canada Inc. (“we”, “us”), a company incorporated in Canada.

This policy explains what personal information we collect through the Nyusie app, the website nyusie.com, and our support emails (together, the “Service”), why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. It applies only to Nyusie, not to any other business of 17774699 Canada Inc.

Our Privacy Officer is Swapnil Arora, Founder, reachable at privacy@nyusie.com. For general help, write to support@nyusie.com.

In short: we use your information to make your briefs, run your account and subscription, and answer you. We do not sell your personal information, we do not show ads, and we do not use third-party analytics. This policy also applies to people testing early versions of the app.

AI processing. Your briefs are written by AI from news reports and read aloud by an AI voice. To write a brief we send our AI and voice providers headlines and short descriptions of news stories and the name of your area. We do not send your name, email address or account details to them. We do not use your information to train AI models, and we use providers whose business terms do not permit them to train on it. These providers may keep what we send them for a limited period, under their own terms, for security and abuse monitoring.

Automated decisions. The app automatically chooses which stories go into each tier based on your area, the places each story involves, and how widely the story is being reported. This shapes your news selection only. It has no legal or similarly significant effect on you.

Waitlist. If you joined the waitlist, we use your email address only to send you one email when Nyusie launches, then delete it. That email includes a way to unsubscribe.

Emails. Apart from that one launch email, we send service emails only. We will not send marketing or newsletters unless you separately opt in, and every such message will include a way to unsubscribe, as Canada’s Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL) requires.

Consent. Where the table says we rely on consent (such as notifications), you give it through your device permission and can withdraw it at any time by turning that permission off. Other uses rely on the legal basis shown in the table. You can stop all use of your information by deleting your account.

5. Where your information is stored

Your information is stored outside Canada, mainly in the United States (Supabase’s East US, North Virginia region). Some providers may process it in other countries. Information stored abroad is subject to the laws of that country, and its courts or authorities may be able to access it.

Before sending information outside Canada or Quebec we assess whether it will be adequately protected, and our contracts with providers require that protection. For people in the EU, UK or Switzerland we rely on the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses, the UK Addendum, or an adequacy decision such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, where a provider is certified.

Account, usage and subscription records While your account is open

When you delete your account, there is a 7-day grace period: sign in again within 7 days and your account is kept. After 7 days, your account is closed, and by the 8th day after your request all of your data, including your briefs, audio, Library and usage records, is permanently deleted from our systems and our providers’ systems, except records the law requires us to keep. Copies in routine backups cannot be accessed and are overwritten within a further 7 days. Briefs already downloaded to your phone stay there until the app’s automatic 7-day clean-up removes them or you uninstall the app. Apple and Google keep their own purchase records under their policies.

7. How we protect it

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS), encrypted storage at our providers, row-level access controls so each user can only reach their own data, and limited staff access. We test the Service’s security before launch and regularly after.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach creates a real risk of significant harm to you, we will notify you and the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (and, where it applies, Quebec’s Commission d’accès à l’information or the EU or UK regulator), as the law requires.

8. Your rights and choices

Wherever you live, you can ask us to do the following, in addition to any other rights your local privacy law gives you:

  • show you the personal information we hold about you, and give you a copy in a common format
  • correct information that is wrong (you can change your last name and email address in the app yourself)
  • delete your information
  • stop using your information for a purpose you previously agreed to

To make a request, email privacy@nyusie.com, ideally from the address on your account; if you no longer have access to it, tell us and we will verify you another way. We may ask you to confirm your identity. We reply within 30 days, and there is no charge.

If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (priv.gc.ca) or the authority where you live (see section 9).

Device permissions. You can turn location and notifications on or off at any time in your phone’s settings. Without precise location, the app uses an estimate based on your IP address.

Deleting your account. Go to Settings → Account → Delete account in the app. You have 7 days to change your mind by signing in again; after that your account is closed and your data deleted as described in section 6. Founding-member status ends when your account is deleted. You can also request deletion without the app at nyusie.com/delete-account, or by emailing privacy@nyusie.com.

Deleting your account does not cancel a Premium subscription. Cancel it first in your Apple App Store or Google Play subscription settings, or you may keep being billed.

9. Additional information for your region

Quebec

Under Quebec’s Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector (Law 25), our person in charge of the protection of personal information is Swapnil Arora, Founder (privacy@nyusie.com). You also have the right to ask for your information to be moved to another organisation in a structured, commonly used format, and to be told about automated processing described in section 3. You can complain to the Commission d’accès à l’information du Québec (cai.gouv.qc.ca).

European Union, European Economic Area, UK and Switzerland

Under the GDPR and UK GDPR, Nyusie is the controller of your personal information. Our legal bases are listed in section 3. In addition to the rights in section 8, you can:

  • object to processing based on our legitimate interests
  • ask us to restrict processing while a concern is resolved
  • receive your information in a portable format
  • complain to your local data protection authority, or in the UK the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk)

Where you gave consent, you can withdraw it at any time; this does not affect processing that happened before.

United States

Where a US state consumer privacy law applies to us (such as those of California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Texas and Oregon), residents of that state have, as that law provides, the right to know what personal information we collect and how we use it, to access, correct and delete it, and to opt out of its sale, sharing for targeted advertising, or profiling.

  • Categories collected in the last 12 months: identifiers (name, email, IP address, device token); commercial information (subscription status); internet or app activity (usage); approximate geolocation (your area’s name); and communications you send us. Sources and purposes are in sections 2 and 3, the recipients are in section 4, and how long we keep each category is in section 6.
  • Sale and sharing: we do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, and have not done so in the last 12 months.
  • Sensitive information: we do not store precise geolocation; when you allow it, your coordinates are used only at the moment a brief is made to find your area (see section 2). We do not collect other sensitive personal information as defined by California law.
  • Non-discrimination: we will not treat you differently for using your privacy rights.

You or an authorised agent can make a request at privacy@nyusie.com. If we decline a request, you can appeal by replying to our decision, and we will answer within 45 days.

10. Children

Nyusie is for people aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child under 16 has an account, email privacy@nyusie.com and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We will post any update here with a new effective date. If a change materially affects how we use your information, we will tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect, and ask for your consent where the law requires it.
